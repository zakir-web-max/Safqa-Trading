Our Story

Founded in 2011, still growing.

Safqa Trading was founded in 2011 by Mr. Abduselam Yassin Abegaz. Over more than a decade, we have built experience in exporting Ethiopian agricultural products to markets around the world.

Our business is built on a strong, long-standing foundation that has earned the trust and reliance of our clients — through quality products and dependable service, year after year.

Vision. To achieve sustainable agricultural export, trading fresh produce worldwide within international standards and procedures, in a cost-effective manner — aligned with the world's growing appetite for fresher, healthier food.

Mission. Safqa Trading is committed to providing quality, fresh, and organic products in response to customer demand, to achieve maximum satisfaction and build a sustainable business.
